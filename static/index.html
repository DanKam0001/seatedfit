What this can't tell you

  • It shows how a garment looks in a generated image, not how it truly fits. It does not simulate fabric or measure a body.
  • On your own photo, the wheel and chair checks use the wheels you mark. A mark in the wrong place gives a wrong answer. We don't check hands or footplates on an upload, so look at those yourself.
  • The garment order is a simple colour contrast calculation, not a style judgement. It says how far apart two colours are, not what suits anyone.
  • The sample people are synthetic, and it has not been tested with wheelchair users yet.
  • YouCam's documentation asks for standing photos. SeatedFit relies on seated use it does not list as supported, which is why every picture is checked.

For human review

    Why a try-on needs a check.

    The try-on is made by YouCam AI Clothes. Its documentation says the photo should be standing, with no sitting or crouching. So when it does work on a seated photo, nobody has promised it will keep the chair intact. SeatedFit checks with plain image measurement, not another AI model.

    1. Wheel clearance

      Compares your photo with the result and finds the garment's new pixels. Reports how much of the worst-covered wheel they hide. On your own photo, it uses the wheels you mark. A mark on nothing wheel-like can't earn a pass.

    2. Wheelchair intact

      Compares wheels, casters and footplates with your photo. If they change to anything but the garment, or a wheel's outline vanishes, the render is refused.

    3. Face and hands

      Your face and hands should stay yours. They are compared with the photo.

    4. Garment applied

      Confirms the picture really changed, so an unchanged photo can't pass as a try-on.

    Colour contrast

    YouCam's AI Face Skin Tone Analysis measures a skin colour from the face region. SeatedFit compares it with each garment's main colour using CIEDE2000 colour difference and sorts the garments by that number. It's a simple contrast calculation, not a style judgement, and it never changes a check result.
